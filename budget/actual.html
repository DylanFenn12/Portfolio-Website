How to read this

Actual bank balance is the real cash on hand as of the last update.
Remaining costs is the budgeted total minus what's actually been paid so far.
Expected dues income is dues still expected to come in.
Expected surplus is current bank balance, plus expected dues income, minus everything still owed (unpaid main expenses + unpaid Additional payments). Amounts already paid aren't counted twice.
